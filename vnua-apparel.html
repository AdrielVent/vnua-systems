Club drops

Merch for two Hofstra engineering clubs I help lead. Each club runs its own preorder campaign so the money goes to the club, and nothing is printed unless enough people order. Nothing goes on sale until Hofstra approves the designs.

Drop 01

The Vnua mark is a 3×3 grid, like one face of a puzzle cube. Each piece in Drop 01 takes one of the cube’s colors. The pieces are designed but not made yet, so the store shows flat sketches instead of photos.
