Why “Vnua”

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade, and I've put it on my work ever since.

Right now that work is a mechanical engineering co-op at Stellant Systems, bringing back Hofstra's SHPE chapter and its AIAA branch, and co-leading ASME. Vnua Connect came out of that: two of my clubs hit the same wall, so I built the tool we needed.

I like things simple. If something here doesn't work yet, it says so.
