Vnua Connect

A home for new clubs, before they're official.

Status
Live
For
Students and officers of new clubs at Hofstra
Runs on
Any phone browser (add it to your Home Screen). An iPhone app is in development.
Built with
HTML, CSS and JavaScript, Supabase, SwiftUI
Links
Open Vnua Connect
Code on GitHub

Why I built it

A new club doesn't show up anywhere until it's official, and two of my clubs hit that wall at the same time. So I built the tool we needed.

What it does

  • Students sign in with a 6-digit code sent to their Hofstra email.
  • Clubs are listed A to Z, never by size or popularity.
  • Events show what's there (free food, swag, beginner-friendly), with RSVP and a QR check-in pass.
  • A club only goes live once its president, VP, treasurer and secretary all accept.
  • Officers see turnout and how many people who RSVP'd actually came, and can export it.
  • Ratings after an event can't be traced back to a person.

Where it stands

It's live, and Hofstra SHPE uses it. The iPhone app is a SwiftUI project that's still in development.

Vnua Connect is student-run. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Hofstra University.
