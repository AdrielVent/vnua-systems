Vnua Lock

A voice log for people who build hardware.

Links
Try the web version

Why I built it

When I'm building something, my hands are busy and I don't stop to write notes. Vnua Lock lets me talk instead, then turns the recording into a work log. I use it for my own co-op and club notes.

What the web version does

  • Records a voice log and transcribes it with Whisper, running inside your browser.
  • Never uploads your audio. Notes are saved only in your browser.
  • Picks a summary, key points and action items out of your own sentences, using simple rules. Nothing is made up.
  • Sorts notes into sections, with tags, date filters and search.

Limits

It's English only, and the small Whisper model mishears some words, so check a note before you share it. The first recording downloads a 40 MB model, so open it on Wi-Fi once first.
