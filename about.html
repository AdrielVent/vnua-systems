About

Vnua Systems is a student-founded company building traceability tools for defense manufacturing.

Founder

I’m Adriel Ventura. I study mechanical engineering at Hofstra. Right now that means a mechanical engineering co-op at Stellant Systems, bringing back Hofstra’s SHPE chapter and its AIAA branch, and co-leading ASME.

Why defense manufacturing

At my co-op I printed four versions of one fixture, changing each based on what the technicians who used it told me. That feedback lived in conversations, not in documents. Vnua Systems is about getting what happens at the machine into the record.

Why “Vnua”

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade, and I’ve put it on my work ever since. Vnua Industries is the parent name; Vnua Systems is the defense work.
