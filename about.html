Why “Vnua”

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade, and I've put it on my work ever since.

What I'm doing now

I study mechanical engineering at Hofstra. Right now that means a mechanical engineering co-op at Stellant Systems, bringing back Hofstra's SHPE chapter and its AIAA branch, and co-leading ASME.

Vnua Connect came out of that club work: two of my clubs hit the same wall, so I built the tool we needed.
