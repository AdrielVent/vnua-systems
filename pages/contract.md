## Why we're doing this

We sent in a proposal for AIAA's Design/Build/Fly competition this year and didn't make the cut. Instead of waiting a year, we're doing something else: a whole semester where two student companies compete for one contract, the way it actually works in defense.

## How it works

- Two teams. Each one is a company with its own idea of how to solve the problem.
- One contract. Both companies go after the same thing.
- Each team writes a proposal and pitches it to the judges.
- The judges pick a winner, and the team that loses finds out exactly why. That's how it works with real contracts, and it's the part you learn the most from.

## What the contract could be

We haven't locked this in yet. Here's the one I'm pitching to the e-board:

> A defense contractor needs a lot of small drone parts made by local machine shops, fast, and it needs proof of how every single part was made.

Why I think that's the right one:

- The Pentagon now treats small drones as something you use up and buy in bulk, not something you keep for years ([Breaking Defense](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/07/hegseth-signs-unleashing-us-military-drone-dominance-memo/)).
- The Defense Department isn't allowed to buy drones or key drone parts made in China ([FY2020 NDAA, Section 848](https://wifcon.com/dodauth20/dod20_848.htm)), so those parts have to come from shops here.
- Since November 10, 2026, those shops can be required to pass a CMMC Level 2 cybersecurity check before they win the work ([Cooley](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2025/2025-09-25-dod-releases-long-awaited-final-rule-implementing-cybersecurity-maturity-model-certification-contract-clause)).

It's a real problem, and there's more than one honest way to go after it. That's what makes it a good contract to fight over.

## My company's pitch

Vnua Systems would help the shops that already exist. Every drawing gets tracked with DatumPane, every build gets logged hands-free with Vnua Lock, and every part ships with a record of how it was made.

The other team's company and pitch are theirs to share.
