## Club merch

I want SHPE and AIAA to have merch people actually wear. Members vote on the design, people preorder what they want, and the club keeps the money. Nothing gets printed unless enough people order it, and nothing goes on sale until Hofstra approves it.

## Drop 01

Six Vnua pieces, each in one color from a puzzle cube, with the Vnua grid on it. They're designed but not made yet, so the store shows sketches for now.
