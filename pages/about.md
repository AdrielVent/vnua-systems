## What I'm doing now

I'm on a co-op at Stellant Systems, where I make engineering drawings, write change notices, and design and print fixtures for the shop floor. At school I brought SHPE back, helped get Hofstra's AIAA branch approved again, and I co-lead ASME.

## Why defense manufacturing

At my co-op I printed four versions of one fixture, changing each one based on what the technicians using it told me. Most of that feedback happened in conversations, not documents. Vnua Systems is about getting what actually happens at the machine into the record.

## Why "Vnua"

Vnua comes from the letters of my last name, Ventura. I came up with it in eighth grade and I've put it on my work ever since. Vnua Industries is the company; Vnua Systems is the defense side of it.
