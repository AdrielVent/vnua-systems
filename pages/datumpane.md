## Why I want to build this

At my co-op I make engineering drawings with GD&T and I've written two engineering change notices. Every time a drawing changes, someone downstream has to figure out what's different before they cut anything. If they miss a change, the part comes out wrong.

DatumPane would put the two versions side by side, list every change, and say what each change means for inspection.

## Why defense shops

Defense parts get built to controlled drawings. As of November 10, 2026, a lot of defense contracts can require a third-party cybersecurity certification called CMMC Level 2 before the contract is even awarded ([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-09-10/html/2025-17359.htm), [Cooley's summary of the dates](https://www.cooley.com/news/insight/2025/2025-09-25-dod-releases-long-awaited-final-rule-implementing-cybersecurity-maturity-model-certification-contract-clause)). So small shops have to keep track of their drawings and protect them too.
