DatumPane

What changed between two revisions of a drawing, and what to inspect because of it.

Status
Concept
For
Machine shops that build parts from customer drawings
Built so far
A walkthrough of one sample drawing
Links
See the bracket example

The idea

When a customer sends a new revision of a drawing, the shop has to find what changed before cutting metal. DatumPane would line the two revisions up, list every change, and say what each one means for inspection.

Why defense shops

Defense parts are made to tightly controlled drawings, and a missed revision can mean a scrapped part or a nonconforming one that ships. Many of those drawings are also controlled information that the shop now has to protect under CMMC.[1]

Where it stands

It’s a concept. I’m talking with shops before I build anything, to find out whether this is a real problem for them and how they handle it today. The DatumPane site walks through one sample bracket drawing so you can see what it would show.
